help_center Common Questions

What is an MCT? expand_more

MCT stands for **Medical Case Token**. It is a unique 12-digit alphanumeric code generated by an approved Hospital. This token allows the NYSC board to securely view your medical report without you needing to carry physical papers.

How long does review take? expand_more

Typically, once an MCT is submitted, the medical board reviews the case within 3 to 5 business days. You will receive an SMS and dashboard notification upon completion.

Can I change my hospital? expand_more

Yes, as long as you haven't submitted your final MCT yet. You can visit any other hospital on the approved list to generate a new token.

Need live support?

Our health desk is available 24/7 for urgent medical relocation queries.
